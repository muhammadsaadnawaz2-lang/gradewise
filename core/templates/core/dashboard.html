Grading rules (4.5 scale)

A+ 4.5 · A0 4.0 · B+ 3.5 · B0 3.0 · C+ 2.5 · C0 2.0 · D+ 1.5 · D0 1.0 · F 0

Relative: A band (A+/A0) goes to at most 30% of the class, B band (B+/B0) to the next 40%, everyone else gets C or lower.

Absolute: your grade depends only on your own total marks (for example 90+ = A+ for everybody who reaches it).

A total below 40 = F. Missing 1/3 of the classes = F, whatever your marks are.
