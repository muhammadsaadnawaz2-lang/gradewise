About Us

We help students understand their grades

GradeWise exists because grading rules are confusing. Weights, attendance limits, class rankings and different GPA scales make it hard to know where you really stand. We turn all of that into one clear picture.

Our mission

Make academic planning simple, accurate and honest

We believe every student should be able to answer three questions at any moment: where do I stand, what grade am I heading for, and what do I need to reach my goal?

GradeWise is built around the 4.5 grading scale and the way universities really grade, with weighted components, attendance rules and both relative and absolute grading.
