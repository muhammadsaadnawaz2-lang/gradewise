Grade scale

Designed around the 4.5 scale

Most GPA tools assume a 4.0 scale and give you the wrong number. GradeWise uses the 4.5 scale by default, and its converter handles 4.0, 5.0, 100-point and 7.0 scales too.

Relative grading, at a glance

A band 30%
B band 40%
C-D 30%

The A band (A+ and A0) goes to at most 30% of the class, the B band to the next 40%, and everyone else gets C or lower.
